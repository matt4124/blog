## Overview 
This simulation was a part of a larger group project within a Renewable Energy unit. Where we were tasked with developing a vehicle-to-grid (V2G) solution. My part of the project was in investigating the ancillary services that can be deployed by V2G technology, in other words how electric vehicles can support and provide stability to the grid. 

I developed a large scale simulation of the grid, the simulation would run a series of power flow solutions in time steps. At every time step the power consumption/ export of each household would be simulated. As well the electric vehicles would be modelled as battery-energy storage systems, their' behaviour would be dictated by the ancillary service being tested.

Below are some parameters of the final grid and simulation. 

| Metric |Value| Notes|

## Key Takeaways

This project provided experience in modelling the interaction between renewable generation, electric vehicles, distributed energy storage, and electricity distribution networks.

A key challenge was bringing together multiple time-dependent components into a single simulation framework. Household demand, PV generation, EV availability, and network power flows all vary over time, meaning that the impact of V2G cannot be understood by examining any one component in isolation.

The project demonstrated how power system simulation can be used to investigate emerging technologies such as V2G and assess their potential impact on future electricity networks.
